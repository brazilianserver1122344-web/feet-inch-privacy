Privacy Policy

App Name: Feet Inch Calculator
Effective Date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Feet Inch Calculator handles user information. We respect your privacy and designed this application to work completely offline without collecting any personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Feet Inch Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data. Specifically:

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account registration is required.
  • No location data, contacts, camera, microphone, or device identifiers are accessed.
  • No analytics, tracking SDKs, or third-party data collection tools are used.

2. Local Data Storage

Your measurement lists, saved calculation history, and selected app theme are stored exclusively on your own device using local browser/WebView storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device, and you can delete it anytime inside the app using the Remove all or Clear All options, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Internet & Permissions

The app functions offline and does not require any special Android device permissions to perform calculations.

4. Children's Privacy

Since the app does not collect any personal information from anyone, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.
